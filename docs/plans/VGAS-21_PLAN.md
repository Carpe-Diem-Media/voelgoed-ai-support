## Session and retention lifecycle

- Staff account transitions follow G005. `INVITED` becomes `ACTIVE` after authorized Entra authentication. Admin disable or a real role change increments generation through the locked action with audit. Repeated disable or an identical role set is idempotent. Authorized reprovision can reactivate `DISABLED` for the same identity. Existing credentials stay invalid. `RETIRED` is terminal.
- The pending OIDC flow is short-lived, registration-bound and single-use. Only a verified callback with the correct local identity can renew the cookie with staff ID, current generation, expiry and registration binding. Wrong identity/tenant, disabled gate, stale generation, wrong binding, bad origin/CSRF and expiry at equality deny. Authorization rechecks current role/scope at protected HTTP, mount, navigation, event and Ash action boundaries. A revoked credential stays invalid after reactivation. Scope and gate changes deny on the next check. Idle connected views terminate at the original deadline.
- Ordinary sign-out with CSRF clears the local cookie and CSRF state. A previously issued copy or an already connected tab may retain access until expiry or administrative revocation. Record the observed result and keep the immediate invalidation decision under VGAS-9. Current local acceptance requires effective denial after admin revocation, expiry and scope loss.
- Preview bytes live only in LiveView private state and the bounded upload process. Apply, replacement/cancel, denial, expiry and crash consume or discard them. Verify cleanup and minimised audit/diagnostics on synthetic data. For retained recovery content G029 permits purge only after dependencies and holds clear. Legal hold requires approval and authorized release. Purged payload is terminal, while minimal dedupe and unresolved delivery references survive. Record what the current app stores, existing exclusions and the approved-policy gap for scheduled cleanup, backups and restoration. Leave cleanup delivery with the VGAS-005 retention/recovery work unless already assigned here.
